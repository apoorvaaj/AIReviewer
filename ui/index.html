Questions about your draft, never answers

This tool reads your IB Physics IA draft and asks you questions about it, one criterion at a time. It never writes, rewrites or suggests wording, so everything in your IA stays your own work.

  • Questions only. Every item quotes your own words and asks one question. Anything that looks like text you could paste into your IA is blocked before you see it.
  • You see what leaves your laptop. Personal details are removed on this computer first. You then see the exact text that would be sent, and nothing is sent until you press Send.
  • Nothing is kept. The app saves nothing except the report and receipt you choose to save. Closing the app clears everything.

Remove personal details

Type the details that could identify you. They are used only to find and hide those words in your draft. They stay in this app's memory and are never saved or sent.

Separate several entries with a semicolon ( ; ). Email addresses, phone numbers and "Name:" or "School:" lines are found automatically.

What was hidden

Untick a term to keep it visible (for example if a surname is also a physics term). Add any other word you want hidden.
